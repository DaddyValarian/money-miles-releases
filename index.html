  1. Download the official APK.

LEARN MONEY MILES

User guide

Set up your first vehicle

Open the vehicle management screen, create a vehicle profile, and enter its name, MPG, fuel price and any optional operating cost per mile. Select the vehicle you want to use.

Calculate an offer

Enter the distance and optional payout. Enable round trip only when the entered mileage represents one direction and you want to include the return. Review the estimate. Accept & Save to keep the trip or Decline & Discard to remove the draft.

Use the floating calculator

Enable the floating calculator and grant Android's display-over-other-apps permission when prompted. Open the bubble while parked, enter your figures and save or discard the offer. Minimize to return to the bubble.

Track mileage with GPS

While parked, grant requested location and notification permissions and start a GPS session. Stop the session when finished. Review GPS mileage separately from estimated trip mileage. Signal gaps may affect accuracy.

Manage trips, expenses and reports

Use trip records to review, edit or delete saved trips. Record expenses in the expenses area. Use reports to review your records and export CSV data. Tap Refresh to retrieve current local records.

Back up and restore your records

Use the backup export option in Reports and save the JSON file somewhere safe. To restore, select the backup using the restore option and review the confirmation. Restoration replaces existing records rather than merging them. Back up current data first.

Install future updates

Park safely and stop GPS. Open App & Membership, tap Check for Updates and follow the official download link. Install over the current app without uninstalling.

WE'RE HERE TO HELP

Support & frequently asked questions

Is Money Miles free?

Yes! Money Miles is completely free during the beta. After the beta, we plan to offer the following subscription prices in US dollars:

  • $2.49 for the first 30 days on the monthly plan, once per new account.
  • $4.99 per month thereafter.
  • $49.99 per year on the annual plan.

Subscriptions are not available yet. You will not be automatically enrolled or charged when the beta ends. Any paid subscription will require your explicit agreement.

How does beta approval work?

After verifying your email, submit your name and wait for approval. Approved access can work offline for up to 24 hours after successful online validation on the same device boot. Rebooting requires online validation. Pending, denied, suspended or revoked accounts cannot use the calculator or GPS, but can view and export local records. Contact support for access questions.

Does it work on iPhone?

The current release is for Android only.

Will my records sync between phones?

No. Starting with v0.37.0, accounts manage beta access only. Driving records do not sync between phones. Use manual backups to transfer records.

Why can't I see the floating calculator?

Check Android's display-over-other-apps permission for Money Miles and enable the floating calculator in the app.

What if installation fails?

Confirm you downloaded the official APK, your device supports the Android version, and your installer has permission. Do not uninstall an existing copy to troubleshoot without backing up your records.

Report issues with your app version, device model, Android version and steps to reproduce. Do not send sensitive records.
