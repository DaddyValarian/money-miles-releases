# Money Miles — Privacy Policy

Effective upon publication. Applies to the free Money Miles beta.

Money Miles is operated by Fuller Snacks (the operator), under its Havena brand. Contact: moneymiles.support@gmail.com.

## Information and purposes
Money Miles processes vehicle details, fuel prices, mileage, trip payouts, expenses, notes and settings to provide calculations and records. Records are stored locally on your device. When you manually start GPS, the app uses precise location, including while tracking in the background, to estimate distance. GPS session records contain distance and session details rather than a saved route. The floating calculator uses display-over-other-apps permission; notifications support tracking and inactivity safeguards.

## Network requests and sharing
The current beta is designed without accounts, cloud synchronization, advertising, analytics or subscription processing. The update checker requests a public version manifest hosted by GitHub. GitHub may receive IP addresses and other technical request information under its own privacy policy. Opening an update link uses your browser. Exporting or sharing files sends them to the destination you choose. If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you provide; we use it to respond and resolve issues. Avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information. We do not sell personal information.

## Retention and choices
Local records remain until changed or deleted, app data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Automatic Android app backup is disabled. You can export your records and manage permissions in Android settings. Exported files are controlled by you and your chosen storage provider. Support correspondence is retained as reasonably needed to resolve requests and meet legal obligations. Contact support to request access, correction or deletion of information held by the operator, subject to applicable law. We cannot remotely retrieve or erase your local records.

## Security and eligibility
We use Android app-private storage and signed updates; no storage or transmission method is guaranteed secure. The beta is intended for drivers aged 18 and older and is not directed to children. Do not use the app while driving in a way that distracts you.

## Changes and contact
We will publish material policy changes before introducing new data practices. Contact moneymiles.support@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests.
