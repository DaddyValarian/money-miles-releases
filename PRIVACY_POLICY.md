# Money Miles — Privacy Policy

Effective upon publication. Account-related provisions apply starting with v0.37.0 of the free Money Miles beta.

Money Miles is operated by Fuller Snacks (the operator), under its Havena brand. Contact: moneymiles.support@gmail.com.

## Information and purposes
Money Miles processes vehicle details, fuel prices, mileage, trip payouts, expenses, notes and settings to provide calculations and records. Records are stored locally on your device. When you manually start GPS, the app uses precise location, including while tracking in the background, to estimate distance. GPS session records contain distance and session details rather than a saved route. The floating calculator uses display-over-other-apps permission; notifications support tracking and inactivity safeguards.

## Network requests and sharing
Starting with v0.37.0, Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore (Google services) process your email address, account identifier, email verification status, submitted name, application and approval status, and administrative timestamps and status-change history. Authentication credentials are handled by Firebase Authentication. We use these services to authenticate users, administer beta access, prevent unauthorized access and maintain administrative records. Google may process technical connection and service information in providing these services. See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy for information about Firebase. Driving records are not uploaded to Firebase. The beta does not include driving-record cloud synchronization, advertising or subscription processing. The update checker requests a public version manifest hosted by GitHub. GitHub may receive IP addresses and other technical request information under its own privacy policy. Opening an update link uses your browser. Exporting or sharing files sends them to the destination you choose. If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you provide; we use it to respond and resolve issues. Avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information. We do not sell personal information.

## Access and offline validation
The app checks approval online and stores a local access validation record for up to 24 hours of offline use on the same device boot. A reboot requires online validation. Approval changes can be detected when connected. Restricted access does not erase local driving records; read-only viewing and export remain available.

## Retention and choices
Local records remain until changed or deleted, app data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Automatic Android app backup is disabled. You can export your records and manage permissions in Android settings. Exported files are controlled by you and your chosen storage provider. Support correspondence is retained as reasonably needed to resolve requests and meet legal obligations. Account and approval records are retained as reasonably necessary to administer access, maintain security and meet legal obligations. Contact moneymiles.support@gmail.com to request account deletion, access or correction. We may verify your identity before processing the request and retain information where necessary for legitimate security or legal obligations. Account deletion may end beta access and does not erase local driving records or exported files. We cannot remotely retrieve or erase your local records.

## Security and eligibility
We use Android app-private storage and signed updates; no storage or transmission method is guaranteed secure. The beta is intended for drivers aged 18 and older and is not directed to children. Do not use the app while driving in a way that distracts you.

## Changes and contact
We will publish material policy changes before introducing new data practices. Contact moneymiles.support@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests.
