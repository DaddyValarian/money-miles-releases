# Money Miles — Privacy Policy

Effective upon publication of this revision. Account-related provisions apply starting with v0.37.0 of the free Money Miles beta; the additional support and access-management features below apply to v0.39.0.

Money Miles is operated by Fuller Snacks (the operator), under its Havena brand. Contact: moneymiles.support@gmail.com.

## Information and purposes
Money Miles processes vehicle details, fuel prices, mileage, trip payouts, expenses, notes and settings to provide calculations and records. Records are stored locally on your device. When you manually start GPS, the app uses precise location, including while tracking in the background, to estimate distance. GPS session records contain distance and session details rather than a saved route. The floating calculator uses display-over-other-apps permission; notifications support tracking and inactivity safeguards.

## Network requests and sharing
Starting with v0.37.0, Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore (Google services) process your email address, account identifier, email verification status, submitted name, application and approval status, applicable access status and expiration, and administrative timestamps and status-change history. Authentication credentials are handled by Firebase Authentication. We use these services to authenticate users, administer beta access, prevent unauthorized access and maintain administrative records. Google may process technical connection and service information in providing these services. See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy for information about Firebase. Driving records, trip payouts and GPS session records are not uploaded to Firebase by the app's account, update or support features. The beta does not include driving-record cloud synchronization, advertising or subscription processing. The update checker requests a public version manifest hosted by GitHub. GitHub may receive IP addresses and other technical request information under its own privacy policy. Opening an update link uses your browser. Exporting or sharing files sends them to the destination you choose. If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you provide; we use it to respond and resolve issues. Avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information. We do not sell personal information.

## Optional in-app support and device information
In v0.39.0, you can send feedback or report a bug through App & Membership. If you choose to submit a message, Firebase stores your account identifier, message, submission time and support review status. Authorized owner administration can review these submissions to investigate issues and improve the app. Do not include passwords, financial details, trip records or other sensitive information.

You can separately choose to share device details through App & Membership. This is optional and is off unless you enable it for your account on that device. When enabled, the app sends the device model, Android version, Money Miles version and a server timestamp for the latest device-details update. Details can be refreshed when you open or return to the app, normally at most once per hour unless you explicitly choose to share again. These timestamps are not continuous location tracking or a log of all app activity. Authorized owner administration uses these details for support and compatibility troubleshooting.

Select Stop sharing device details to stop future uploads from that installation and request removal of the stored profile. If removal fails, the app asks you to retry when online. If you have enabled sharing on another installation, disable it there too. You can also contact support for help removing information. Device sharing is not required for beta access.

## Access and offline validation
The app checks approval and applicable membership access online and stores a local access validation record for up to 24 hours of offline use on the same device boot, or until an assigned expiration if sooner. A reboot requires online validation. Access status changes can be detected when connected while the app or an active driving service is in use. Online changes may not reach a disconnected device until it reconnects or its existing local validation expires. Restricted access does not erase local driving records; read-only viewing and export remain available.

## Retention and choices
Local records remain until changed or deleted, app data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Automatic Android app backup is disabled. You can export your records and manage permissions in Android settings. Exported files are controlled by you and your chosen storage provider. Support correspondence and in-app feedback are retained as reasonably needed to resolve requests, investigate issues and meet legal obligations. Shared device details remain until replaced, removed through the sharing control, or deleted in response to a verified request, subject to necessary legal or security retention. Account, approval and access administration records are retained as reasonably necessary to administer access, maintain security and meet legal obligations. Contact moneymiles.support@gmail.com to request account deletion, access, correction or removal of submitted feedback or shared device information. We may verify your identity before processing the request and retain information where necessary for legitimate security or legal obligations. Account deletion may end beta access and does not erase local driving records or exported files. We cannot remotely retrieve or erase your local driving records. Account-related information held by the service is separate from those local records.

## Security and eligibility
We use Android app-private storage and signed updates; no storage or transmission method is guaranteed secure. The beta is intended for drivers aged 18 and older and is not directed to children. Do not use the app while driving in a way that distracts you.

## Changes and contact
We will publish material policy changes before introducing new data practices. Contact moneymiles.support@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests.
